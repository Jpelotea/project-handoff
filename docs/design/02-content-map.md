# Content map

## Page journey

1. **Hero** — identifies the audience, names the outcome, and presents one primary CTA.
2. **Problem** — makes chat, document, and ownership fragmentation concrete.
3. **Workflow** — explains capture, run, and close-the-loop in three steps.
4. **Benefits** — connects the workflow to visible ownership, reusable context, less status chasing, and repeatable delivery.
5. **Use cases** — grounds the concept in agency campaign delivery and consultancy reporting/advisory work.
6. **Waitlist form** — requests only the information needed to qualify concept interest.
7. **FAQ** — answers availability, audience, cost, data collection, and analytics-consent questions.
8. **Final CTA and footer** — repeats the conversion and concept disclosure.

## Conversion path

Visitor → hero or contextual CTA → waitlist form → accessible validation → Netlify submission → thank-you page → GA4 `generate_lead` after confirmed success and only with analytics consent.

## Supporting routes

- `/privacy/` — collection, analytics, purpose, choice, and contact.
- `/thanks/` — submission confirmation with noindex metadata.
- `/404/` — recoverable error route with noindex metadata.
