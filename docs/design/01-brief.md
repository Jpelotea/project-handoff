# Project Handoff — Product brief

## Concept boundary

Project Handoff is a fictional B2B SaaS portfolio concept. It demonstrates a complete landing-page delivery workflow without presenting fabricated customers, traction, testimonials, or commercial performance.

## Audience

Small agencies and consultancies with approximately 5–30 team members that deliver recurring client work.

## Problem

Recurring client delivery is fragmented across chat threads, documents, and individual memory. Context is hard to retrieve, ownership becomes ambiguous, and teams spend time chasing status rather than moving the work forward.

## Value proposition

> Turn recurring work into clear, accountable handoffs.

## Primary conversion

Join the private beta. The form collects a work email, team-size range, primary workflow challenge, and explicit contact consent.

## Trust and evidence constraints

- Use: “Private beta · Free to join · No credit card.”
- Label the product as a fictional portfolio concept in the FAQ, footer, privacy notice, consent copy, and README.
- Do not use invented customer logos, testimonials, user counts, conversion improvements, or product performance claims.
- Do not send email addresses, team sizes, workflow answers, or other form values to analytics.

## Technical scope

Astro static output, custom CSS, vanilla JavaScript, Netlify Forms, and consent-aware GA4 measurement.
